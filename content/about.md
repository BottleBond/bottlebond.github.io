## About BottleBond

BottleBond started with a simple belief: bourbon and whiskey appreciation should be accessible to everyone. Whether you're taking your first sip or you've been collecting for decades, there's always something new to discover in the world of American whiskey.

Our podcast combines rigorous research with genuine enthusiasm, bringing you deep dives into history, honest tasting notes, and conversations with the people who shape the industry.
