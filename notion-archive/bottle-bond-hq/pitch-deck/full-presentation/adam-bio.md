Adam is a tech enthusiast by day and an avid gamer by night. During the transformative period of early 2020, he sought out new interests that required nothing more than what his home could offer. Embracing the newfound freedom from daily commutes, Adam discovered a passion for Bourbon, which quickly became his favorite hobby. Initially, this interest complemented his gaming sessions, but by mid-2024, Adam realized he could merge this newfound love with another passion: travel.

This revelation sparked a fervent curiosity and an active pursuit of Bourbon's rich heritage. Adam has since embarked on journeys across 10 states and even ventured to Japan, seeking unique finds and perfect pairings.

Join us as Adam delves deeper into the fascinating history and culture of his beloved hobby, uncovering the stories and traditions that make Bourbon truly special.
