Core audience will likely be Millennials and Gen X with an average activity time of 40 minutes per session, some average to above average level of education and a bias towards reviews and/or educational content

- **Age and Gender**:
    - Whisky enthusiasts often fall within the 30-55 age range and tend to be predominantly male, though there is a growing interest among women and younger audiences in premium and craft spirits.
- **Income and Education**:
    - Given that whisky, especially premium brands, is a luxury item, listeners are likely to have higher disposable incomes and an interest in finer details about their beverages, aligning with the general podcast listener demographic trends.
- **Listening Habits**:
    - Whisky podcast listeners might prefer episodes that delve into the history, production, and tasting notes of different whiskies. Given the trend towards higher education and income, these listeners likely appreciate detailed, well-researched content.
