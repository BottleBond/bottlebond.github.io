Framing:

We will talk a bit about the Tariffs, but really we don’t want to go to deep.  The core take away after all of our research, is that the issue is nothing like the headlines read.  It’s postured as a trade negotiation, but it’s really a wrench thrown at a plethora of other issues from allegations of drug trafficking and border control issues, to attempts at leveling some of the trade deficits that we share with many of our largest trade allies.

Why does this all suck?  We had a long running deal called NAFTA, that was later replaced by USMCA.  The theme of all of these are core capitalism ideals.  Free trade, so we can let the markets drive the demand and profit.  We turned on a dime and instead implemented massive and sweeping tariffs (with multiple false starts), with literally no room to discuss or negotiate.  We started with threats and decided to work it out after the fact.

The other reason this is triggering to a lot of people on both sides of the aisle - The claim was that we were going to reduce the footprint of government, get it out of the way of honest hard working Americans so they can just live the American dream.  Instead - we’ve attacked our allies and broken a trust that took generations to build; so far the net result has been a massive decrease in Canadian tourism to the US (On source suggests estimates as high as $3bil/year; another points out a decrease of over 5% in flight bookings from Canada to the US on US carriers, and 1.1% on Canadian carriers).  While it’s really too early to speculate on the total impact; it is clear that this has soured relations and looks to be a massive backfire from the originally published goals.
