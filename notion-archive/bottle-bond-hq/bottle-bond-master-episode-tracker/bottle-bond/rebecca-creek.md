# Rebecca Creek

**Tasting Pour #1: Rebecca Creek Bottled in Bond**

- **First Impressions:** Aroma, color, initial tasting notes.
- **Origins of Rebecca Creek Distillery:**
    - Founded by **Steve Ison** 2009, with a vision for **premium Texas spirits**.
    - Named after **Rebecca Creek**, known for **limestone-filtered water** in the Texas Hill Country.
    - One of the **largest craft distilleries in Texas**, now distributed in **17 states**.
    - One of the **early pioneers of craft distilling in Texas**.
    - Competing with brands like **Garrison Brothers, Balcones, and Still Austin**.
- **How it Blends Tradition & Innovation:**
    - **Traditional copper pot stills** - **Christian Carl - 1000 gallon**
    - Double Distilled
    - Proofed to 120, then transfered to be Aging in **#4 char American oak barrels** for depth of flavor.
    - Aging - 4-7 years
